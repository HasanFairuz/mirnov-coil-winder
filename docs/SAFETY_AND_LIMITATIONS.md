# Safety, Limitations and Publication Boundaries

[← README](../README.md)

## Hardware safety

This winder is an academic laboratory prototype, not a production-qualified machine. Spindles, couplings, traversing nozzles, motor drivers and power wiring can present entanglement, pinch, puncture and electrical hazards. Operators should use validated guarding, electrical packaging and emergency-stop architecture consistent with their laboratory requirements. Do not treat the archived firmware as safety-certified controls.

## Technical limitations

- Open-loop motion, with no independently verified spindle/traverse encoder feedback.
- No automatic missed-step or wire-break detection.
- Manual bobbin loading, threading and initial wire fastening.
- Wire tension primarily passive; no demonstrated closed-loop force regulation.
- Calibrated winding region and wire gauge specific to tested configurations.
- Quantitative turn accuracy, winding spacing, cycle-time distributions and endurance are not independently established by this public archive.
