# Mechanical Design and Fabrication

[← README](../README.md)

The team designed and assembled an aluminum-extrusion tabletop fixture with separate winding and feed subsystems. A motor-driven spindle rotates the bobbin. An MGN9 guided carriage translates a wire nozzle through a lead screw and anti-backlash nut. Ceramic guides manage wire routing; the final report describes passive spool resistance as the principal tension source and felt tensioning as optional supplemental adjustment.

## Important elements

- Bobbin holding and spindle shaft alignment
- Traverse motor, lead screw, carriage, limit switch, and endpoint calibration
- Ruby-tipped final wire guide and ceramic eyelets
- Printed brackets, covers, electronic mounts, and frame connections
- Operator access for threading, bobbin installation, and post-cycle removal
